DecoTreeMod allows you to add outdoor decorative elements in the form of trees to the game. You can choose between trees planted in wooden boxes or decorative artificial trees, which you can place wherever you like.

Mod Details:

This mod adds 6 types of decorative trees, available in wooden boxes and with flowers:

Palm Tree Box
Oak Tree Box
Redwood Tree Box
Birch Tree Box
Fir Tree Box
Cactus Box

To craft one of these decorations, you must be a farmer and go to the agriculture table. There, you will find the necessary recipes. A wooden box and agricultural items will be required for crafting. Additionally, a carpenter must be present to complete the recipe.

You can also choose to have these trees in their natural versions, without flowers or boxes. These can be crafted directly at the agriculture table:

Palm Tree
Oak Tree
Redwood Tree
Birch Tree
Cactus

Usage:

The usage is very simple. The decorations can be crafted at the agriculture table.

Installation:

Place the folder "DecoTreeMod_YOURLANGUAGE" in the "/Mods/UserCode" directory and also add the "Assets" folder to "/Mods/UserCode".
