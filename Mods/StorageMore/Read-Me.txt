Mod details:


The Storage Mod adds 14 new storage items to the game. Here’s the list:

Pallet (Crafted at the Carpenter's Table):
Allows storage of all construction items and ingots, with a capacity of 8 slots, stackable up to 80.



Big Bag (Crafted at the Tailor's Table):
Allows storage of dirt, sand, stone, and crushed materials, with a capacity of 10 slots, stackable up to 80.



Wood storage units in 3 different sizes (Crafted at the Carpenter's Table):

SmallStorage: 18 slots, stackable up to 40.
MediumStorage: 24 slots, stackable up to 60.
LargeStorage: 32 slots, stackable up to 80.



Containers in 3 different sizes and capacities (Crafted at the Mechanics Table):

Blue Container: 32 slots, stackable up to 60.
Green Container: 64 slots, stackable up to 60.
Red Container: 128 slots, stackable up to 60.




Shelves to store all in-game items (Crafted at the Mechanics Table):

Small Single Shelf: 8 slots, stackable up to 40.
Small Double Shelf: 16 slots, stackable up to 40.
Large Single Shelf: 32 slots, stackable up to 40.
Large Double Shelf: 64 slots, stackable up to 40.



Wall-mounted tool storage (Crafted at the Carpenter's Table):

Allows storage of all tools in dedicated slots, with a capacity of 32 slots.




Usage:

The usage is very simple. Each storage item can be crafted from the table mentioned above. You can also find each item in the Ecopedia.


Modification : 

Annotations have been added to the code in the StorageMore_English.cs file, allowing you to adjust the storage size, stack multiplier, and other options as you wish.


Installation:

Place the "StorageMore_YOURLANGUAGE" folder in the "/Mods/UserCode" directory and also add the "Assets" folder to "/Mods/UserCode".
